`zarr.create` and `zarr.api.asynchronous.create` now list `mode` and `data` in their signatures and docstrings. Both were accepted before without being declared. An unknown keyword now raises a `TypeError` from `create` before anything in the store is touched. Previously, with `mode="w"`, the existing data was deleted before the error was raised. `mode` is documented as a legacy spelling; `overwrite` is preferred.
