Encoding a chunk through a numcodecs filter no longer passes the
filter's flattened output to the next codec: `_encode_sync` reshapes the
flattened array back to the chunk shape, so downstream codecs
(e.g. `TransposeCodec` or an `imagecodecs` serializer chained after a
`Delta` or `FixedScaleOffset` filter) receive a chunk of the expected
shape instead of a flat 1-D array that could make writing fail or
silently corrupt data. Shape-changing codecs such as `PackBits` are
unaffected.
