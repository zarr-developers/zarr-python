`WrapperStore`, and so `LoggingStore`, now forwards `getsize`, `getsize_prefix`, `get_ranges_sync` and `supports_consolidated_metadata` to the store it wraps. Before this change, a wrapper read a whole value to measure its size, and reported support for consolidated metadata even when the wrapped store reported none. `LoggingStore` now also logs range reads (`get_ranges` and `get_ranges_sync`).
