The Zarr-Python 2 keyword arguments `synchronizer`, `chunk_store`, `cache_metadata`, `cache_attrs`, `object_codec`, `read_only` and `meta_array` accepted by `zarr.create`, `zarr.group` and `zarr.open_group` (and the functions that wrap them, such as `zarr.zeros`) have never had any effect in Zarr-Python 3. Passing one of them now emits a `ZarrDeprecationWarning` instead of a "not yet implemented" `ZarrRuntimeWarning`. They will be removed in a release after April 2027. Calls that do not pass these arguments are unaffected.
