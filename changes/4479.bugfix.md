Opening an array with a rectilinear chunk grid, and indexing it, now costs time and memory proportional to the size of the grid's run-length encoded metadata rather than to its number of chunks: a stored grid such as `[[1, 1000000000000]]`, a few hundred bytes of JSON, previously took seconds per ten million chunks to open and could exhaust memory. The explicit edges in `RectilinearChunkGridMetadata.chunk_shapes` and `VaryingDimension.edges` are now held as `zarr.core.common.RunLengthEdges`, an immutable run-length encoded value that is not a sequence: it has no `len`, indexing or iteration and does not compare equal to a `tuple`. Its `expand()` method yields one entry per chunk. `VaryingDimension.cumulative` is removed; `VaryingDimension.chunk_offset` gives the start of a chunk. The repr of a chunk grid shows a dimension of more than 100 chunks in its run-length encoded form.
