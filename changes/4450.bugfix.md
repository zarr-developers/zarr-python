`ZipStore` now opens its archive on first use from every method, so calling `get`, `get_partial_values`, `set_if_not_exists`, or `clear` on a store that was never opened no longer raises `AttributeError`. A store created with `mode="w"` or `mode="x"` now keeps its entries when it is reopened by `move()` or by unpickling, instead of truncating the archive or refusing to open it.
