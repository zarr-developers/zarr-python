Nested structured dtypes now round-trip through Zarr format 2 metadata. Structured dtypes with field titles or subarray fields now raise `ValueError` instead of losing field information. Aligned, padded, and explicitly offset layouts remain accepted and are converted to packed layouts with a `ZarrUserWarning`: field values are preserved when writing arrays, although offsets and itemsize may change. Use `numpy.lib.recfunctions.repack_fields(data, recurse=True)` to make this conversion explicit. These checks also apply to nested fields. The `zarr.testing.strategies` module gains `zdtypes` and `structured_dtypes` strategies, with property tests for dtype serialization and warned layout normalization.
