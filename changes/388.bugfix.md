A stored shard too short to hold its shard index, including a zero-length value, now raises a `ValueError` saying the shard is truncated or corrupt, on every read and partial-write path under either codec pipeline. Previously the asynchronous full-shard read and partial write treated a zero-length shard as missing (reading the fill value, or overwriting it), while every other path failed with a checksum mismatch or a decode error. zarrs and tensorstore also reject such a shard.
