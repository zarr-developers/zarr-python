The `lazy_indexing_numpy` and `lazy_indexing_dask` examples are plain scripts that call their functions from `main()`, so running them no longer needs pytest. They ran their functions through `pytest.main` with a `-c` argument that pytest 9.2 rejects, which failed the package's test suite under pytest 9.2. `tests/test_doc_examples.py` now runs them in process like the other examples, and skips the Dask example where `dask` or `zarr` is not installed.
