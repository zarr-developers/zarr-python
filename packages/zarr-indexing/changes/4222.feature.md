Fancy selections on `LazyArray` and `IndexTransform` now compose without
restriction: a second `oindex`/`vindex`/mask step may land on any axis of an
already-fancy view, including axes an existing index array merely broadcasts
along. Array-carrying transforms are chained with `compose` instead of being
rewritten in place, and resolution handles the resulting mixed, correlated, and
diagonal index-array structures through a shared pointwise path (classified by
the new `index_array_structure`). This also fixes a crash where a slice-only
`vindex` step (`view.lazy.vindex[...]` or `view.lazy.vindex[..., scalar]`)
after a correlated gather misclassified the gather as orthogonal and failed at
`result()`.

Also: `LazyArray.result(parts=...)` now raises `ValueError` (not
`AssertionError`) when supplied parts do not tile the view; `with_parts` and
`with_parts_per_axis` raise the documented `ValueError` for non-iterable input;
and `__dask_tokenize__` digests the view's canonical transform body instead of
embedding it, keeping tokens small for large fancy selections.
