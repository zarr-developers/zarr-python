The v2 codec checks now accept the configurations that numcodecs 0.15 and
0.16 write and read back: a zstd `level` outside -131072 to 22, which
zstd clamps, a negative quantize `digits`, a shuffle `elementsize` of 0,
a negative blosc `blocksize`, a blosc `shuffle` of `true` or `false`,
`"astype": null` for delta, fixedscaleoffset and quantize, and a checksum
`"location": null`. A codec's dtype parameter is now checked by the same
rules as an array's dtype, so `<f3`, `<i16` and `|f4` are problems there
too.
