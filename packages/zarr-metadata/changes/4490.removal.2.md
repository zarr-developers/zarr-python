Documents the specs do not allow are refused where they were accepted.
A `must_understand: false` is refused at every extension point, codecs
too. An extension name must match `^[a-z][a-z0-9-_.]+$` or be a URI;
any other name, such as `""` or `"foo/bar"`, is an `invalid_value` at
`name`. The `regular` chunk grid refuses a chunk length of 0, along a
dimension of length 0 too. A group's `consolidated_metadata` of `null`
is an `invalid_type`. A `.zarray` with a structured dtype and
`fill_value: 0` is refused; the spec requires base64 or null. Readers of
stores zarr-python 3.0 and 3.1 wrote can use the repair readers.
