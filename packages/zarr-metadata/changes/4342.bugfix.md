Added `numpy_datetime64_configuration` and `numpy_timedelta64_configuration` validators (and the shared `numpy_time_unit`) to the `numpy.datetime64` and `numpy.timedelta64` leaf modules. They enforce the constraints the configuration TypedDicts cannot express: `scale_factor` must be an integer in `[1, 2**31 - 1]`, the `generic` unit must have `scale_factor` 1 because NumPy's generic time type carries no scale, and the equivalent `μs` spelling of the microsecond unit is normalized to `us`.
