A scope's `definitions_of` and `claimant` now raise `TypeError` for a kind of the other Zarr format, instead of answering that the scope holds none. Type checkers now reject such a kind in `definitions_of` and in `field_json_schema_v3`. `ZarrV3Kind` and `ZarrV2Kind`, in `zarr_metadata.v3.definition` and `zarr_metadata.v2.definition`, name the kinds of each format.
