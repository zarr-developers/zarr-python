`NodeName` and `NodePath`, in `zarr_metadata.v3`, are the names and paths
of the nodes of a v3 hierarchy, modelled on zarrs' types of those names,
and `zarr_metadata.model` judges a string by the spec's rules for them:
`validate_node_name_v3`, `is_node_name_v3` and `parse_node_name_v3`, and
their `node_path` twins.
