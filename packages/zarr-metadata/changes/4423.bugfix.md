A `bytes` or `bytearray` is not a JSON array. Where a document expects an
array, an empty one passed as an array with no items:
`parse_array_metadata_v3` returned `storage_transformers=b""`, and the v2
readers took `dtype=b""` and `filters=b""`, then failed when the model was
written. The validators and `parse_*` now refuse one at `codecs`,
`storage_transformers`, `dimension_names`, a v2 `dtype` and its field
records, and v2 `filters`.
