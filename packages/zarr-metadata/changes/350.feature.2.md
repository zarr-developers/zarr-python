Every extension the package models is a definition in
`zarr_metadata.v3.definition`'s terms: the codecs `blosc`, `bytes`,
`cast_value`, `crc32c`, `gzip`, `scale_offset`, `sharding_indexed`,
`transpose` and `zstd`; every data type, the raw-bytes `r<N>` family
included; the `regular` and `rectilinear` chunk grids; and the `default`
and `v2` chunk key encodings. Each is a constant in its own module --
`BLOSC_CODEC`, `STRUCT_DATA_TYPE` -- whose configuration is the TypedDict
that module already declared and whose rules are a function over it. A
shard's pipelines, a cast's target type and a struct's field types are
nested fields, annotated `CodecField` and `DataTypeField`, and read in
the scope their field is read in. `CORE` holds what the specification
defines and `CORE_AND_EXTENSIONS` adds what `zarr-extensions` registers.

`canonicalize(field, kind, scope)` gives a field in its simplest
equivalent spelling: the configuration its TypedDict admits, at any
depth, then its definition's own `canonical`, each nested field in its
own simplest spelling, and the envelope in the fewest words -- the bare
name when nothing is configured, and no `must_understand`. What
`canonical` gives is judged again, and one that does not hold is a
`ValueError`, a fault in the definition.
