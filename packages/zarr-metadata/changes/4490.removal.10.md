The `validate_*`, `is_*` and `parse_*` functions for whole documents and for one field are removed from `zarr_metadata.model`: those for `array_metadata_v2`, `array_metadata_v3`, `group_metadata_v2`, `group_metadata_v3` and `metadata_field_v3`. To get every problem in a document, call its reader, such as `read_array_metadata_v3(document).problems`; to get the model or an error, call the model's `from_json`; to check one field, call `zarr_metadata.definition.resolve`. `ZarrV3ArrayMetadataJSONPartial`, `ZarrV3GroupMetadataJSONPartial`, `ZarrV2ArrayMetadataJSONPartial` and `ZarrV2GroupMetadataJSONPartial` are removed too; to name the members of a document to change, use the model's `...Update` TypedDict, such as `ZarrV3ArrayMetadataUpdate`.
