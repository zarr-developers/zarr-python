An array checked against a union of arrays of fixed lengths is checked only
    against the branches of its own length. A v2 struct record of neither
    length is reported as "expected an array of 2 or 3 elements", where the
    message named only the three-element form, and a two-element record with
    a name that is not a string, `[5, "<f4"]`, is reported at the name
    instead of as a record of the wrong length.
