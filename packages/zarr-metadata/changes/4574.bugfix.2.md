**Breaking:** The v2 readers now agree with the v3 readers. A `.zarray` member the spec does not define may hold `NaN` and the infinities, a `.zmetadata` key that does not name its node's path, such as `/a/.zarray`, is refused instead of being read as the node at `a`, and a `.zgroup` that holds `attributes` is an `invalid_value` rather than an `unknown_key`, so a caller who tolerates unknown keys no longer drops those attributes.
