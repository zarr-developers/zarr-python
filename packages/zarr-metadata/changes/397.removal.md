The v2 models are no longer dataclasses built from typed members: `ZarrV2ArrayMetadata(shape=..., dtype=..., ...)`, `dataclasses.replace` and `dataclasses.fields` no longer work on them; build one from a document with `ZarrV2ArrayMetadata(document)` or `from_json`, and change one with `update`. `ZarrV2ArrayMetadataPartial` and `ZarrV2GroupMetadataPartial` are replaced by `ZarrV2ArrayMetadataUpdate` and `ZarrV2GroupMetadataUpdate`, whose members are JSON as a document writes them. A `.zarray` with a structured dtype and `fill_value: 0` is refused; the spec requires base64 or null.
