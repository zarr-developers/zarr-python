**Breaking:** The v2 readers agree with the v3 readers in three places. A
    `.zarray` member the spec does not define may hold `NaN` and the
    infinities, as a v3 member that nothing claims may, in a store and in a
    `.zmetadata` alike. A `.zmetadata` key that does not name its node's
    path, such as `/a/.zarray` or `a//b/.zarray`, is refused, as a v3
    listing refuses such a key, instead of being read as the node at `a` or
    `a/b`; zarr-python looks an entry up by its node's path and never finds
    one written otherwise. A `.zgroup` that holds `attributes` is an
    `invalid_value`, as a `.zarray` that holds them is, not an
    `unknown_key`, so a caller who tolerates unknown keys does not drop the
    attributes it holds.
