`zarr_metadata.model.read_openable_node_metadata_v3` reads a v3 `zarr.json` as `read_node_metadata_v3` does, and also reports each declaration a reader must understand to open the node that nothing in scope claims, such as an unknown codec or an unknown top-level member without `"must_understand": false`, as a problem of the new kind `unclaimed_declaration`. Its reading has no problems exactly when a reader that understands the scope can open the node, so a validator does not have to check `unclaimed_declarations` itself. The other readers still accept unclaimed declarations, and the `from_json` and reader docstrings now point to `unclaimed_declarations` and the new reader.
