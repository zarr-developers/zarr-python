The validation boundary says what the package decides where the specs
leave it open or settle it two ways: `attributes` may hold `NaN`,
`Infinity` and `-Infinity`, which `to_key_value` writes as bare tokens;
`must_understand: false` is refused at every extension point, codecs
too; a chunk length of 0 is allowed along a dimension of length 0. The
models' `from_json`, `to_json`, `from_key_value` and `to_key_value`
have docstrings, and no public docstring names a private function.
