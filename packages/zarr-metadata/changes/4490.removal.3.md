**Breaking:** a v2 datetime or timedelta dtype without a unit, `<M8` or `<m8`, is now a
problem, because the v2 spec requires the unit in brackets, as in
`<M8[ns]`. zarr-python 3 writes these dtypes for a NumPy `datetime64` or
`timedelta64` with no unit, and 0.5.0 accepted them. A codec's dtype
parameter, such as the `dtype` of a `delta` filter, may still leave out
the unit, as numcodecs writes it.
