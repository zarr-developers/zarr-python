**Breaking:** a v2 datetime or timedelta dtype without a unit, `<M8` or `<m8`, is now a problem, because the v2 spec requires the unit in brackets, as in `<M8[ns]`. zarr-python 3 writes these dtypes for a NumPy `datetime64` or `timedelta64` with no unit, and 0.5.0 accepted them. A codec's dtype parameter, such as the `dtype` of a `delta` filter, may still leave out the unit, as numcodecs writes it. To read such a document, rewrite the dtype with the unit its data was written in, such as `<M8[ns]`, or read it in a scope whose `update` replaced the `datetime64` or `timedelta64` definition with one that accepts it.
