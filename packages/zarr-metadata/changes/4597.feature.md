Added strategies to `zarr_metadata.testing.strategies` for the definitions that hold other declarations: `struct`, `cast_value` and `sharding_indexed`. Their nested declarations are drawn from the same `Strategies` mapping, so a struct's field types, a cast's target data type and a shard's codecs come from every definition the mapping holds. A new `pipelines` strategy draws a whole codec pipeline for a `Chunk`, drawing each codec for the chunk the codec before it hands on, as that codec's `transition` gives it. Shards are drawn at most one inside another, and structs likewise. Every function a strategy record holds now also takes the `Strategies` mapping as its last argument, and `declarations` reads what it draws in the scope of every definition the mapping holds.
