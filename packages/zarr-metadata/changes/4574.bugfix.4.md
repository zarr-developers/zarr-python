A v2 struct record of the wrong length is reported as "expected an array of
    2 or 3 elements", where the message named only the three-element form. A
    two-element record whose name is not a string, `[5, "<f4"]`, is reported
    at the name, where it was reported as a record of the wrong length. The
    checker behind `zarr_metadata.typed_json.check` tries an array only
    against the union branches of its length, and names a union of arrays of
    fixed lengths by those lengths.
