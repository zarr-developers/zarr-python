Two v2 codecs that differ only in a parameter that one writes and the
other leaves to its numcodecs default are now one codec, so their arrays
compare equal: `{"id": "zstd", "level": 0}` equals
`{"id": "zstd", "level": 0, "checksum": false}`. This holds for every
default that numcodecs documents, such as a gzip `level` of 1, a checksum
`location` of `"start"` and a delta `astype` equal to its `dtype`, and a
blosc `shuffle` of `true` equals 1. Values that numcodecs reads alike are
one value too: a dtype parameter of `<i1` equals `|i1`, a
fixedscaleoffset `scale` of 10 equals 10.0, and a blosc `typesize` is
ignored when `shuffle` is 0.
