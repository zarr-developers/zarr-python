Two v2 codecs that differ only in a parameter that one writes and the
other leaves to its numcodecs default are now one codec, so their arrays
compare equal: `{"id": "zstd", "level": 0}` equals
`{"id": "zstd", "level": 0, "checksum": false}`. This holds for every
default that numcodecs documents, such as a gzip `level` of 1, a delta
`astype` equal to its `dtype`, and a checksum `location` of `"start"`, or
`"end"` for crc32c. A blosc `shuffle` of `true` equals 1. A dtype
parameter of `<i1` equals `|i1`, which numcodecs reads alike.
