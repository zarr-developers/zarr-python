Two declarations that hold other declarations, such as two `sharding_indexed` codecs, are now equal when they differ only in the order their configurations write their members in. The declarations a configuration holds were compared in the order it writes them, so a shard that writes `index_codecs` before `codecs` was not equal to one that writes them the other way round, and neither were the array models that hold them, although the order of the members of a JSON object means nothing. The items of an array, such as the codecs of a pipeline, are still compared in order.
