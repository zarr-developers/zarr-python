The public doors are smaller. `zarr_metadata.v3.definition` and `zarr_metadata.v2.definition` no longer export the scope algebra's helpers (`claims_of`, `claim_key`, `refines`, `Claims`, `ClaimKey`, `Disagreements`), the canonical-spelling and field-walking helpers (`canonical_of`, `canonicalize`, `configuration_of`, `fields_of`, `with_problems`, `chunk_grid_lengths`, `read_pipeline`, `field_json_schema`), `check` (still in `zarr_metadata.typed_json`) or `WithFillValue`; a model's `claims` and a scope's `disagreements` are no longer public. `zarr_metadata.model` no longer re-exports the `*_KEYS_*` sets, `is_json`/`parse_json`/`validate_json`, or the JSON shapes of known writer bugs. `zarr_metadata.typed_json` no longer exports `typeddict_keys`. What a document, a model, a definition, a scope and a reading are is unchanged.
